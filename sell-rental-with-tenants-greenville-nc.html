An occupied rental can still be sellable

Some landlords assume the only path is to end the tenancy, renovate, and list. An investor buyer may instead purchase subject to an existing lease when the tenancy and property make sense. This can reduce vacancy, turnover work, and showings.

Information that helps us price the rental

  • Current lease term and monthly rent.
  • Payment history and any outstanding balance.
  • Security-deposit amount and records.
  • Utilities, maintenance obligations, and recent repairs.
  • Property condition and reasonable access for review.

Tenant and deposit obligations continue through a sale

A sale does not erase the lease or the need to handle tenant funds correctly. Lease rights, notices, security deposits, and possession issues should be handled according to the agreement and North Carolina law. The closing attorney can coordinate transfer documentation when appropriate.

When a direct rental sale makes sense

Landlords often contact us because of repeated repairs, turnover, management fatigue, a difficult property, deferred maintenance, or a desire to simplify a portfolio. For broader landlord situations, see our tired landlord guide.
