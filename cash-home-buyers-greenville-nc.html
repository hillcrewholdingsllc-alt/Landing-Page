Frequently asked questions

Can I sell my Greenville house as-is?

Yes. We evaluate properties in their current condition. You do not have to complete repairs, updates, staging, or a full cleanout before asking us to review the house.

Do you buy inherited houses in Greenville?

Yes. We evaluate inherited and estate-owned homes throughout Greenville and Pitt County. The ability to complete a sale depends on ownership, estate authority, title, and the facts of the particular property.

Will you buy a Greenville rental with tenants?

We evaluate occupied rentals as well as vacant ones. Existing leases, tenant rights, condition, timing, and the seller's goals all matter in determining whether a direct purchase works.

Do you only buy houses inside Greenville city limits?

No. Greenville is our core market, but we also evaluate properties throughout Pitt County, including Winterville, Ayden, Farmville, Grimesland, Simpson, and surrounding communities.

Is a cash sale always better than listing?

No. A traditional listing can make more sense for a seller whose priority is maximum market exposure and who has the time and resources to prepare the property. A direct purchase is another option for owners who place more value on condition flexibility or simplicity.
