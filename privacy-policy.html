Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

K Real Estate & Investment LLC ("K Real Estate," "KREI," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit kbuyhouses.com, submit information about a property, communicate with us, or otherwise interact with our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, telephone number, email address, property address, information about a property, seller situation, and other details you choose to provide through our website, phone, text message, email, or other communications.

When you use our website, we may also collect technical and attribution information such as your IP address, browser or device information, referring page, landing page, website activity, analytics identifiers, advertising click identifiers, and UTM campaign parameters. These may include identifiers associated with advertising platforms such as Google or Meta when applicable.

How We Use Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and evaluate properties for a potential purchase or other real estate transaction.
  • Communicate with you about your property or request.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and internal business systems.
  • Measure advertising performance, lead quality, and marketing attribution.
  • Maintain records of inquiries, communications, offers, transactions, and business activity.
  • Prevent spam, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Advertising and Analytics

We may use analytics and advertising services, including services provided by Google and Meta, to understand website usage and measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These services may use cookies, browser storage, advertising click identifiers, or similar technologies. Information from advertising interactions may be associated with subsequent lead and transaction outcomes for internal attribution and performance measurement.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product. We may share information with service providers and business systems that help us operate our business, such as website hosting, automation, analytics, advertising, communications, CRM, data processing, and professional service providers. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, prevent fraud, or complete a transaction you have requested or authorized.

Text Messages and Communications

If you provide a telephone number and communicate with us by text message or request that we contact you, we may use that number to respond regarding your inquiry or property. Message and data rates may apply. You may ask us to stop text messages by replying STOP where applicable. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from us.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining business and transaction records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update or correct information you previously provided. You can also control certain cookies or tracking technologies through your browser or device settings. Disabling some technologies may affect website functionality or measurement.

Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may link to or use third-party websites and services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults involved in real estate matters and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
