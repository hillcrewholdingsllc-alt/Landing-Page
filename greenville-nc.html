Why Greenville sellers consider a cash home buyer

A direct buyer is not the best solution for every homeowner. Sellers who want maximum market exposure may prefer listing with a licensed real estate broker. A direct purchase can make more sense when a house needs major repairs, an inherited property needs to be settled, tenants or vacancies complicate a sale, foreclosure or taxes create a deadline, or the owner values a simpler transaction.

Because we are buying the property rather than marketing it for you, our offer reflects the home’s current condition, expected repairs, local market value, transaction costs, and project risk. You can compare that offer with your other options and decide what works best.
