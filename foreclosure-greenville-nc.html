Need to evaluate selling before foreclosure?

If mortgage payments are behind or a foreclosure proceeding has started, timing matters. A property sale may be one option when there is enough time, equity, and legal ability to close before the foreclosure process reaches its final stage.

First determine the actual foreclosure stage

Missed payments, a notice of default, a filed foreclosure, a hearing, and a scheduled sale are different stages. Do not rely on a generic internet timeline. Review the documents you received and verify the current reinstatement or payoff amount with the servicer or trustee.

A sale requires enough time to close

If you choose to sell, the buyer, closing attorney, lender or servicer, and title process all have to be coordinated before the applicable deadline. Earlier contact gives more room to verify payoff figures, inspect title, and solve issues without last-minute pressure.

Information that helps us evaluate quickly

  • Property address and condition.
  • Mortgage statement and approximate balance.
  • Any reinstatement or payoff quote.
  • Foreclosure notices, hearing date, or scheduled sale date.
  • Other liens, taxes, HOA balances, or title issues you know about.

A direct offer is one option—not legal or foreclosure advice

We buy real estate; we do not provide legal advice or promise to stop a foreclosure. If the house fits our criteria and the transaction can close in time, a sale may allow valid liens and the mortgage payoff to be handled through closing.
