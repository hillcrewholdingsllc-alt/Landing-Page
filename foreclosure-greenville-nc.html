Need to evaluate a sale while foreclosure is pending?

When a property is behind on payments or involved in a foreclosure proceeding, timing matters. A direct sale may be one option worth comparing if there is enough time and the ownership can legally convey the property.

Why sellers contact us

Owners sometimes contact us because they want to understand whether selling the property could be an alternative to continuing through the foreclosure process. The property may need repairs, there may be limited time for showings, or the owner may simply prefer a direct transaction if the numbers and timeline work.

We can review the address, condition, ownership, and available timeline. If the property fits our criteria, we can discuss a direct purchase offer. Whether a closing can actually happen before a scheduled hearing or sale depends on the specific case and the professionals involved in the closing.

Information to gather early

  • Property address and current owner names.
  • Any known hearing, sale, or trustee dates.
  • Current lender or servicer information.
  • Approximate mortgage payoff or arrears, if known.
  • Whether the property is owner-occupied, tenant-occupied, or vacant.
  • Any liens, judgments, HOA claims, or other known title issues.

You do not need every document before reaching out, but accurate dates are especially important. We do not invent urgency or assume that a published date is still active without verification.

Direct sale versus listing

A traditional listing can provide broader market exposure and may produce a higher price, but it can also require more time, property preparation, buyer financing, inspections, and contingencies. A direct investor sale can trade some of that market exposure for a simpler process and potentially faster execution. The right choice depends on your equity, timing, property condition, and goals.
