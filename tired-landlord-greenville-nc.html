Signs it may be time to compare a sale

Repeated maintenance, large capital repairs, poor cash flow, long-distance ownership, tenant turnover, or simply wanting to redeploy capital are all legitimate reasons to test the market. A direct offer gives you another number to compare with keeping or listing the property.

Occupied and vacant rentals

We review both. For an occupied rental, the lease, rent history, deposits, and tenant situation matter. For a vacant rental, we focus more heavily on current condition, needed repairs, and realistic rental or resale economics.

Sell before another renovation cycle

If the house needs flooring, paint, HVAC, roof work, plumbing, electrical updates, or general deferred maintenance, you can ask for an as-is review before spending money on another turnover. We price the work into our offer rather than requiring you to complete it first.

What we need to review the property

Send the address, current rent or vacancy status, lease details if occupied, major known repairs, and your preferred timeline. We can usually determine quickly whether the property fits our acquisition criteria.
