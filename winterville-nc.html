How a direct sale works

Start by sharing the address, your contact information, and what is going on with the property. We review the details and follow up personally. If the property fits our buying criteria, we can make a direct offer for you to consider. There is no obligation to accept it.

We are based in the Greenville area and focus on Pitt County. That lets us evaluate Winterville properties with local context while keeping the process simple for the seller.

When selling as-is may make sense

A direct sale can be useful when the property needs more work than you want to take on, when you inherited a house you do not plan to keep, or when timing matters more than preparing for a traditional listing. We can review residential properties throughout the Winterville area.
