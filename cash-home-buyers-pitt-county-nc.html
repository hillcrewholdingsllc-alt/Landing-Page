Questions? Frequently asked

How quickly will I get an offer?

After we receive the property details and photos, we aim to review and respond promptly with next steps. The timing to complete a full written offer depends on the property's complexity; we will explain the expected schedule when we contact you.

How do you calculate offers?

Offers are based on recent local sales, the property's condition, estimated repair costs, carrying costs and the local resale or rental market. We provide a clear explanation of the major inputs when we present an offer.

Do I need an inspection before you buy?

Not always. We often start with photos and information. We may request a walkthrough or inspection as a condition of the final offer when necessary.

Will you pay closing costs?

Responsibility for closing costs varies by transaction. We discuss who pays which closing fees in advance and include details in any written offer.

Can I sell if the house has liens or unpaid taxes?

Possibly. Liens and unpaid taxes affect title and payoff requirements. We work with title companies and your advisors to clarify what's needed. For authoritative information about local tax procedures, contact Pitt County or the NC Department of Revenue.

Should I get legal or tax advice?

Yes. For legal, tax, probate, or foreclosure matters we refer sellers to an attorney or the relevant North Carolina agencies. For probate resources see the NC Judicial Branch (https://www.nccourts.gov/local-courts).
