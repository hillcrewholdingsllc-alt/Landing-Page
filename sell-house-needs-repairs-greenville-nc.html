Compare the cost of repairs with an as-is sale

Preparing a house for the traditional market can require cash, time, contractors, cleanup, and uncertainty. Depending on the property, repairs may include roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical work, flooring, paint, kitchens, bathrooms, structural work, or simply years of deferred maintenance.

A direct as-is sale gives you another option. We review the property in its present condition and account for the repairs ourselves when determining whether we can make an offer.

Properties we commonly review

How a direct offer works

We consider the property’s location, current condition, comparable sales, expected renovation costs, transaction expenses, and project risk. If it fits our criteria, we can discuss a direct purchase. There is no obligation to accept an offer, and you can compare the direct-sale option with repairing and listing the house.
