Real estate investing in Greenville and eastern North Carolina.

K Real Estate & Investment LLC is a Greenville-based real estate investment company focused on acquiring and improving properties in Pitt County and surrounding eastern North Carolina markets.

Our approach to real estate investment

We evaluate opportunities based on the property, location, condition, ownership situation, rehabilitation needs, market demand, and realistic exit options. That can include houses needing repairs, rental properties, inherited or estate-owned real estate, vacant houses, and land.

Our work is acquisition-focused. We purchase properties for our own account rather than acting as a seller's real estate broker. Owners who want to discuss selling can start with our Greenville home-selling and cash-offer page.

Greenville, NC investment properties

Greenville and Pitt County include a mix of owner-occupied housing, student and conventional rentals, older housing stock, infill opportunities, and land. East Carolina University, ECU Health, regional employment, and population movement all influence local housing demand, but each property still requires individual underwriting.

We do not treat every Greenville property as the same investment. Acquisition price, repair scope, rent or resale demand, title, occupancy, taxes, insurance, and holding costs can materially change a project's economics.
