What a direct cash sale actually means

A direct sale is different from putting a property on the open market. We evaluate the house for our own investment account, account for its current condition and the work or risk we would take on, and make an offer if the property fits our criteria. You do not pay us an agent commission because we are the buyer, not your listing broker.

A cash offer is not automatically the highest possible price. Its value is usually in convenience, reduced preparation, fewer showings, and a simpler path to closing. Sellers should compare price, certainty, timing, repairs, commissions, concessions, and closing requirements—not just one headline number.

How we determine whether we can make an offer

We look at location, comparable sales, current condition, repair scope, occupancy, title or lien issues, and the resale or rental economics of the property. We may ask for photos or schedule a walkthrough when needed. Our goal is to make the review specific to the property rather than giving a generic online estimate.

What happens next?

Send the property address and a few details. We review the property, ask only the questions needed to understand the situation, and determine whether a direct purchase is a fit. If it is, you can compare our offer with your other options. There is no obligation to accept.
